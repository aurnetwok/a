About AURA Network

AURA Network is a decentralized digital token ecosystem built to ensure secure peer-to-peer microtransactions with daily community mining rewards.

Our infrastructure delivers effortless token transfers, instant wallet assignment, and transparent transaction management for everyday users.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. AURA Network stores encrypted session records solely to facilitate mining cycles and account authentication.

We do not share your private data with third parties. All backend operations run strictly via secure cloud infrastructure.

Terms of Service

By registering on AURA Network, you agree to comply with daily fair mining standards. Double-mining or automated script exploit attempts will result in account suspension.
