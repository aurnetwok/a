About AURA Network

AURA Network is a next-generation decentralized digital token ecosystem built to facilitate seamless peer-to-peer microtransactions with daily community mining rewards.

Our core architecture delivers effortless token transfers, automated instant wallet key generation, and transparent transaction tracking designed for mass adoptability.

Core Objectives

  • Frictionless peer-to-peer microtransactions
  • Fair community-driven token distribution
  • High scalability with ultra-low protocol fees

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and identity security are paramount. AURA Network stores encrypted session records solely to facilitate continuous mining cycles and verify account ownership.

We strictly adhere to zero-data-sharing protocols. Personal records, balances, and communication logs are never sold or shared with external third-party advertisers.

All backend processes operate over end-to-end encrypted infrastructure ensuring data integrity and user confidentiality.

Terms of Service

By registering an account on AURA Network, you explicitly agree to comply with our fair-use protocol guidelines. Exploitation attempts using scripts, automated bots, or duplicate accounts will result in immediate termination.

AUR token transfers automatically activate once your global wallet balance reaches the baseline threshold of 100 AUR.

AURA Network reserves the right to adjust reward schedules to guarantee ecosystem longevity and stability.
